Course 01

IT Concepts and Tools

Developing confidence with digital tools, productivity software and the professional responsibilities involved in working in IT.

Course overview

This course introduces the role of IT in organisations and the effective use of common digital tools. My learning has included professional conduct, legal and ethical responsibilities, data communication and spreadsheet skills.

Skills developed

  • Using productivity tools to organise and present information.
  • Working with spreadsheet formulas, functions and data summaries.
  • Understanding privacy, copyright and responsible digital communication.
  • Communicating information clearly and professionally.

Learning evidence

  • Excel exercises involving formulas, functions and PivotTables.
  • Exploration of spreadsheet grouping and summarising data.
  • Learning activities on copyright, privacy, accessibility and unsolicited communication.

Reflection

This course has helped me understand that IT involves more than using technology. It also requires choosing appropriate tools, presenting information clearly and considering the legal and ethical impact of digital work. Spreadsheet activities have helped me become more comfortable working with and summarising data.
